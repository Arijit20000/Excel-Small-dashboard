# Interactive Sales & Seller Performance Dashboard (Excel)

An interactive Microsoft Excel dashboard built using **Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, Slicers, and Timelines** to analyze computer hardware sales (`₹ 40,87,950` total revenue | `1,434` units sold) across cities, months (Jan–Apr 2025), products, and sales representatives.

## Key Insights
- **Top Performing City:** **Sitapur** generated the highest revenue at `₹ 15,43,150` (~37.7% of total sales), followed by Lucknow (`₹ 13,40,900`) and Lakhimpur (`₹ 12,03,900`).
- **Monthly Trend:** Sales peaked in **March** (`₹ 11,08,400`) after a dip in **February** (`₹ 9,26,100`).
- **Top Sellers:** **Shivam Verma** (`₹ 7,73,950`) and **Gajendra Singh** (`₹ 7,69,200`) led overall sales performance across 6 representatives.
- **Highest Volume Products:** **SSD** (`263` units) and **RAM** (`248` units) recorded the highest quantity sold across 6 hardware categories.

## Workbook Structure
- **`Data`**: Raw transactional dataset (`S.N`, `Seller`, `City`, `Date`, `Item`, `Qty`, `Rate`, `Total`).
- **`Pivot Chart`**: Backend Pivot Tables driving all dynamic metrics and visual summaries.
- **`Dashboard`**: Executive overview featuring Total Sales KPI, Qty of Items Sold, City-Wise Sales, and Month-Wise Sales trends.
- **`Seller Dashboard`**: Representative-level performance breakdown filtered by Seller, City, Item, and Date.

## Tools & Features Used
- **Microsoft Excel**: Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, Interactive Slicers (`City`, `Item`, `Seller`), Timeline Filter (`Date`), and Excel Tables.
